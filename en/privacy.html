Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Pocket Voice values your privacy and complies with applicable laws.

1. Data We Collect

  • No account signup is required, and we do not collect direct identifiers such as name, email address, or phone number.
  • Person names, photos, voice recordings, categories, sort order, and widget selections that you add are stored on your device and in the App Group container used by the widget.
  • App settings such as language and app volume are stored on your device.
  • We do not operate a server and do not collect or view this information.

2. iCloud Auto Backup

  • If you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive turned on, your people, photos, and voice recordings are backed up automatically to your own iCloud account.
  • The backup is used only to load your data on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • Data in iCloud is managed by Apple, and the developer cannot access it. iCloud data is handled under the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • The backup uses your iCloud storage.

3. In-App Purchases

  • Pocket Voice Plus is purchased through Apple's App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details or Apple Account information.
  • Your purchase status and the app version you first installed (used to grant Plus to early users) are checked on your device using information Apple provides, and are not sent anywhere.

4. Purpose of Use

  • Provide person list management, voice recording, and playback
  • Show selected photos and names in Home Screen widgets and play voices from the widget
  • Back up to iCloud and load your data on other devices
  • Check access to Pocket Voice Plus
  • Restore your settings when reopening the app

5. Permissions

  • Microphone: Used to record voice clips (up to 10 seconds, or 1 minute with Pocket Voice Plus).
  • Photo Library: Used to let you choose photos for people and widget backgrounds.
  • Widgets and App Group: Used so the app and widget can access the same photos, recordings, and person data.
  • iCloud: Used to back up and load your data in your own iCloud.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • Data on your device is removed when you delete a person in the app or delete the app.
  • A person you delete is removed from the iCloud backup at the next backup. To delete the whole backup, go to Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage and delete the Pocket Voice data.

7. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties, except where required by law.

8. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 14.

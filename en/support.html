FAQ

The widget doesn't play sound.
Check your iPhone's silent mode and volume, and the App Volume in the app's Settings. Also touch and hold the widget, tap "Edit Widget", and make sure a person is selected.

I bought Pocket Voice Plus but it isn't on.
Open Settings → Pocket Voice Plus → Buy, then tap "Restore Purchase". Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I'm moving to a new iPhone.
Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both devices and turn on iCloud Drive. Open and close the app on your old iPhone to back up, then open the app on your new iPhone to load everything automatically.
